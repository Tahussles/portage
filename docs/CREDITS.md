# Credits

## Landing visuals
- No stock footage or photos. The hero is the Portage line (`public/topo-hero.svg` plus an inline route), and the chapter images in `public/chapters/` are screenshots of Portage itself, captured by `scripts/gen-chapter-shots.mjs`.

## Map
- `public/geo/canada-provinces.json`: Natural Earth admin-1 states and provinces, 1:50m (public domain), generated by `scripts/gen-geo.mjs`.

## Topography
- `public/topo.svg` and `public/topo-hero.svg` (the landing hero's calmer contours): generated from seeded simplex noise by `scripts/gen-topo.mjs`.
